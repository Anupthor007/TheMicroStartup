# Campus Skill Loops

**Author:** Anup  
**Date:** 2025-02-06  
**Tags:** #tech #community #business #youth #education

---

## Overview
A micro-marketplace that connects college students with nearby local businesses for small, recurring digital services. Students offer micro-skills (design, editing, social media posts, simple dashboards), and businesses choose fixed-scope service packages (“loops”).

---

## Problem Statement
- Students in tier-2/3 colleges have useful creative and technical skills but lack consistent opportunities for paid work.
- Local businesses like cafes, gyms, hostels, tiffin services, coaching centres need:
  - posters, reels, WhatsApp creatives,
  - simple websites,
  - basic data tracking.
- These businesses cannot afford agencies and don’t know whom to hire.
- No simple system exists to connect **local student talent** with **local business needs**.

---

## Proposed Solution
A hyperlocal system around one campus (1–2 km radius):

- Students list fixed-scope “Skill Loops”:  
  - Social Media Loop (12 posts + 4 reels/month)  
  - Launch Loop (branding + posters + reels for a new cafe)  
  - Data Loop (simple sales/attendance dashboards)
- Businesses browse and pick loops instead of negotiating custom work.
- Everything runs small-ticket, recurring, and local — easy to start and maintain.
- Initial version can run on Forms + Notion/Sheets + WhatsApp before building a platform.

---

## Implementation Plan

### Phase 1 (0–2 weeks)
- Create student intake form (skills, availability, samples).
- Create business request form (type of work, budget comfort).
- Maintain two shared sheets:
  - Student Services
  - Business Requests
- Define 3–5 standard loops.
- Start pilot with:
  - 10–20 students  
  - 5–10 nearby businesses

### Phase 2 (after validation)
- Build a simple dashboard for:
  - matching student → business  
  - tracking deliverables  
  - rating/feedback

### Phase 3 (scaling)
- Create a city playbook for other colleges.
- Add AI helpers for captions, templates, dashboards.

---

## Resources Needed
- Notion / Google Sheets
- Two forms (student + business)
- Basic templates for posts, reels, posters, dashboards
- One coordinator (could be student volunteer or workspace owner)
- Optional: workspace for student creators (like Atulya’s space)

---

## Expected Outcomes
- Students earn recurring micro-income.
- Local businesses get affordable digital help.
- A replicable “Campus Skill Loop” model across cities.
- A youth-driven micro-economy around tech + creativity.

---

## Open Questions
- How to maintain quality and consistency when students have exams?
- Should pricing be fixed or flexible by city?
- How to ensure businesses don’t request work outside the loop?
- Should the coordinator earn a fixed fee or percentage?
