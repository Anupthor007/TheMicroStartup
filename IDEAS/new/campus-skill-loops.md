# Campus Skill Loops

**Tags:** #tech #youth #education #india  
**Stage:** Idea

---

## 1. Problem

- College students in tier-2/tier-3 cities have skills (design, coding, video editing, social media, basic ML) but:
  - don’t know how to get small paid work,
  - rely only on random internships or unpaid work.
- Local businesses (cafes, coaching centers, gyms, hostels, tiffin services) need:
  - posters, reels, WhatsApp creatives, simple websites, menu designs, basic data tracking,
  - but can’t afford agencies or don’t know whom to hire.

There is no simple, local bridge between *student talent* and *nearby businesses*.

---

## 2. Core Idea

A **micro marketplace for one campus + 1–2 km radius**:

- Students list **simple, fixed-scope services** (e.g., “5 Instagram posts/week”, “one-page website”, “poster pack for a month”, “simple analytics dashboard for sales tracking”).
- Local businesses pick from pre-defined “loops” instead of negotiating from scratch.
- Everything is **small-ticket, recurring, and local** → easy to start, easy to maintain.

This can start completely manual: one Notion/Sheet + WhatsApp group.

## 4. Revenue Loop (₹3–4L/month target)

For a single city / cluster:

- Each business pays small, recurring retainers:
  - ₹2k–₹5k/month for simple packages.
- If:
  - 30 businesses pay avg ₹3k/month → ~₹90k/month GMV
  - 50–80 businesses over time → ₹1.5–₹2.5L/month GMV
- Platform keeps **10–20% fee** for:
  - matching,
  - QA of work,
  - templates + guidance,
  - community + workspace access.

Students keep the rest — sustainable for both sides.

---

## 5. MVP (0–2 weeks)

No heavy tech. Start ultra-simple:

1. **Create a form** for students:
   - skills, availability, sample work links.
2. **Create a form** for local businesses:
   - type of work needed (social media, poster, website, data tracking).
3. Maintain 1–2 shared Notion/Google Sheets:
   - one sheet for “Student Services”,
   - one sheet for “Business Requests”.
4. Create **3–5 fixed “loops”** (packages), e.g.:
   - Social Media Loop: 12 posts + 4 reels/month,
   - Launch Loop: posters + menu + reels for new cafe launch,
   - Data Loop: basic sales/attendance tracking in Google Sheets + charts.
5. Start with:
   - 10–20 students,
   - 5–10 businesses near campus.

Once there is demand, you can build a simple web dashboard.

---

## 7. Risks / Open Questions

- Quality control: how to ensure businesses are happy with student work?
- Consistency: students have exams, burnout, or move away.
- Pricing discipline: keeping offers simple instead of custom chaos.

These can be addressed with:
- clear templates,
- backup students per business,
- very strict scope per package.
